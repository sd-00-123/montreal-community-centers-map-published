Map of Montreal Elderly Community and Recreation Centers

Map Function: Map of Montreal showing elderly community and recreation places, bus stops, and transit routes. Use the map controls to zoom and move around the map. Select a point or route to view more details.

Enables seniors to access community resources and services in Montreal through a visual platform.

This map is for The elderly, their loved ones helping them find the information they need, workers / voluteers at recreational / community centers, and anyone else needing this map for visual representation of centers.

What you can do here: Use the map to identify the location of recreation and community centers and plan transit to these locations accordingly. With this map, you can easily find the nearest centers and plan your route. This helps ensure that seniors can access the resources and services they need. Having all the information is in one place, it's easier to find and use.
